# career_runtime

## Purpose
Track the player's office career state through existing runtimes: title, performance, career experience, salary cycle, promotion eligibility, lightweight skills, and daily career feedback.

## Invariants
- No top-level Career Manager, Career Engine, Career Repository, Salary Manager, or Promotion Manager.
- New players default to `intern` / `实习生`.
- Missing old-save career fields migrate to default `CareerState`.
- Career experience only increases and is source-id deduped.
- Performance stays within `0..100`.
- Ordinary performance deltas are small; major deltas are capped.
- Salary is paid at most once per seven-day period.
- Promotion is deterministic, ordered, and never random.
- Skill requirements can gate higher promotions but must remain queryable and explainable.
- Career feedback is generated by daily simulation and kept short, specific, and non-punitive.
- UI must not calculate performance, salary, skills, feedback, or promotion.
