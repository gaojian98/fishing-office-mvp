# Product Experience Map

## Office Hub

Purpose: let the player understand today's office in a few seconds.

Top layer:
- Current date and time.
- Weather and festival context.
- Office mood with product explanation.

Second layer:
- Activity, productivity, social level, tension.
- Active resident and group counts.

Third layer:
- Recommended actions.
- Player reputation.

## Resident List

Purpose: find who is around and who can be interacted with.

Shows:
- Name.
- Current location.
- Current activity.
- Mood.
- Friendship stage.
- Filter and sort controls.

## Resident Detail

Purpose: understand the resident and choose a gentle interaction.

Priority order:
- Basic identity.
- Current state.
- Available interactions.
- Personality.
- Relationship.
- Visible/private profile hints.
- Memory, stories, rumors, cooldowns.

## Interaction Feedback

Purpose: explain what happened without turning the interaction into a number dump.

Shows only groups with actual changes.

## Share Fish

Purpose: make fish inventory a social topic, not a forced reward flow.

Visible fields:
- Selected fish.
- Rarity.
- Weight.
- Quantity.
- Resident preference or unavailable reason.
- Confirm action near the top of selector.
